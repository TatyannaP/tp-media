Our vision includes

Community, Education & Opportunity

Our vision is to use media, education, events, and community-building to help people understand technology and media, discover opportunities, develop skills, and build meaningful connections, especially people who haven't always seen themselves in these spaces.

Our mission

Educate. Connect. Create access.

Media is how we reach people. Online and in real community spaces, we want to help everyday people understand technology and media, find a way in, and meet others on the same path.

Educate

Plain-language education on AI, technology, and media. We're exploring workshops, educational media, and learning resources, from AI literacy to content creation.

Connect

Workshops, networking nights, creator and entrepreneur meetups, panels, and community events, in person and online. This is future programming, not a current schedule.

Create access

Many paths into tech and media are simply poorly explained. We want to be a trusted guide to apprenticeships, workforce programs, bootcamps, certifications, scholarships, and internships that already exist. We don't run these programs; we help people find and understand them, and may develop our own later.

How we work

Philosophy

Clarity over clutter. Quality over quantity. Story first, always. We believe content built with intention outlasts content built for volume.

Approach

Thoughtful, collaborative, and intentional. We're building slowly and in the open — because the work deserves it, and so does the audience.

Focus

Media, storytelling, creators, culture, technology, education, and community. These aren't separate lanes — they're the same conversation happening in different rooms.

Longer-term vision. We're also exploring a mentorship or membership community that could connect emerging professionals, creatives, entrepreneurs, and people entering technology with peers, experienced professionals, learning resources, and opportunities. It's an idea we're exploring, not a launched program.

Tatyanna

Media Producer · Host · Content Strategist

Many interests aren't a lack of direction. They're the map.

TP Media is founded by Tatyanna, a Tampa native whose path runs through media, music and entertainment, entrepreneurship, community work, and technology.

She has interviewed artists, worked around music and entertainment, created podcast and other content, built businesses, shown up in nonprofit, networking, and event spaces, and eventually moved into technology. Today she works with data, Python, automation, and AI.

For years, having so many interests felt like not having one clear direction. TP Media is the realization that they were never disconnected. Media, creativity, technology, community, and storytelling belong in the same room.

She knows what it's like to enter technology through a nontraditional path, to feel imposter syndrome, and to learn new skills as an adult while building confidence along the way. That's why she cares about making intimidating spaces feel open. She's also a mother, and that perspective shapes why she's building something meant to last.

Rooted in Tampa

Built here, shaped by here.

TP Media is being built in Tampa and influenced by the people around it: the artists, entrepreneurs, technologists, organizers, and neighbors doing real work in this city. We want to tell their stories and build alongside them.
